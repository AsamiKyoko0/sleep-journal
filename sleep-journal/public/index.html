How it works

Three steps to better sleep

Takes less than two minutes each morning. That's it.

1
Log last night
Enter your sleep and wake time, tap what woke you up (noise, stress, heat…), check off your good and bad habits, and rate your quality from 1–5.
2
Watch your week
The This Week tab shows your duration chart, trend line, weekly goal progress, and any patterns the app has spotted in your data.
3
Act on the insights
After each log, personalised tips appear based on exactly what woke you up that night — not generic advice, but targeted to your situation.
4
Keep your data
Export everything as a CSV at any time. Your sleep history belongs to you — not us.
